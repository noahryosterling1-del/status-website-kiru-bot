Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. Information We Collect

KIRU may process information required to provide its Discord bot features. This can include Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, command configuration, moderation settings, and other information necessary for configured bot features.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate KIRU, provide requested commands and server features, maintain configurations, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.

3. Data Storage

KIRU only stores information that is reasonably necessary for its features. Data may be removed when it is no longer required or when the associated configuration is removed.

4. Third Parties

KIRU may use Discord and other services required to provide specific features. Information handled by those services is subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Requests

If you have a question about information associated with KIRU or want to request deletion of applicable stored data, please contact the KIRU support team through the official support server.

6. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when KIRU's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. Continued use of KIRU after an update means you have access to the revised policy.
